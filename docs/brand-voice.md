# Propella brand voice

Source: *PROPELLA Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines* (client, 2026). This file is the
working version for anyone — person or model — writing copy in this repo.

Every string a student reads is covered: UI labels, empty states, errors,
notifications, emails, and the AI assistant's system prompt.

## Who we are talking to

Students sitting JAMB, WAEC and NECO, roughly 15–22. Ambitious, often
overwhelmed, worried about failing, looking for clarity rather than another
textbook. Parents are a secondary audience — with them, be professional and
reassuring.

## The test

> "Would the smartest senior student or tutor I wish I had say it this way?"

Calm under pressure. Patient. Encouraging without sounding fake. Confident
without showing off. Never makes anyone feel stupid for asking.

## Voice

- **Encouraging** — celebrate effort, not just results. Build confidence instead
  of fear.
- **Simple** — everyday language, short sentences. If a secondary school student
  would not understand it, simplify it.
- **Intelligent** — knowledgeable, never academic or robotic. We teach; we do not
  lecture.
- **Human** — students are people, not users. Acknowledge stress, celebrate
  progress.
- **Optimistic** — every message should leave the reader more confident than
  before they read it.

## Every message earns its place

It must do at least one of: teach something, encourage someone, solve a problem,
build confidence, create curiosity, inspire action. If it does none, rewrite it.

## Say / don't say

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| Study smarter. | Study harder. |
| Let's figure it out together. | You're doing it wrong. |
| You've made progress. Keep going. | That's easy. |
| Every question helps you improve. | You should already know this. |
| One step at a time. | Last chance! Don't fail! |
| You're closer than you think. | Only geniuses pass JAMB. |

## Words we avoid

**fail · impossible · genius · guaranteed · miracle · cheat · last chance ·
secret hack · overnight success**

We build confidence. We never manipulate it.

## Grammar and style

Active voice. Short sentences. Simple English. Explain before you impress. No
educational jargon. Do not exaggerate. Never use fear to market.

## Emoji

Intentional, never excessive. None in formal announcements or official
documents. The product UI uses icons rather than emoji.

## What this means in the product

Concretely, when writing strings in this codebase:

- **Empty states** say what will appear here and how to make it appear —
  "Nothing scheduled today. Enjoy the breather, or pick a topic and get a head
  start." Never "You have not studied anything."
- **Errors** put the fault on the system, not the student — "Propella is not
  responding. This is on our side, not yours."
- **Quiz feedback** treats a wrong answer as information — "Every question helps
  you improve", never "Wrong" alone.
- **Marathon and streaks** never shame a broken streak or an abandoned run. A
  run that was stopped early is simply not congratulated; it is not scolded
  either.
- **Countdowns** state the days left plainly. No "Only 3 days left!!"

## Before you publish

Is it clear? Is it encouraging? Is it accurate? Does it help the student? Would
our ideal tutor say this? Does it sound like Propella?

If any answer is no, revise.
